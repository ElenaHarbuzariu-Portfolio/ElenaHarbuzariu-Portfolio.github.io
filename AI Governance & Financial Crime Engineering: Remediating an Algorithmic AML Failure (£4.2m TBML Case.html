AI Governance & Financial Crime Engineering: Remediating an Algorithmic AML Failure (£4.2m TBML Case Study)

Deploying machine learning into anti-money laundering operations without human capacity guardrails, explainability, or deterministic overrides can actively degrade compliance defenses. At ApexGlobal Bank, the uncalibrated rollout of OmniShield AI generated 14,200 alerts within 90 days—overwhelming first-line teams, compressing average investigation time from 45 minutes down to a dangerous 8 minutes, and allowing a complex £4.2m Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML) scheme to move undetected.

To remediate the regulatory breach and establish defensible AI governance across the Three Lines of Defence, I architected a closed-loop six-document control framework uniting Acceptable Use policy boundaries, Three Lines of Defence governance, deterministic TBML detection engineering, a 45-minute Tier-1 investigation playbook, a risk-stratified retroactive QA lookback audit, and a 60-day shadow-mode validation protocol.
