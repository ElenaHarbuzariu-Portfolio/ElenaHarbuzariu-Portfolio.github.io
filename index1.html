AI Governance & Financial Crime Compliance Risk Case Study: Remediating an Algorithmic AML Failure (£4.2m TBML Case Study)

Deploying machine learning into financial crime operations without capacity guardrails, explainability, or deterministic overrides can actively weaken compliance defenses. In this capstone scenario at ApexGlobal Bank, the uncalibrated rollout of OmniShield AI generated 14,200 alerts within 90 days—overwhelming first-line analysts, compressing average review times from 45 minutes down to a dangerous 8 minutes, and allowing a complex £4.2m Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML) scheme to move unreviewed.

To remediate the control breakdown and establish defensible oversight across the Three Lines of Defence, I developed an integrated six-document governance and operational control suite translating principles from the EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and PRA SS1/23 into concrete AML policies, deterministic rule specifications, analyst playbooks, QA lookback audits, and shadow-mode validation gates.
